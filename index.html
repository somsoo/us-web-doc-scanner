📖 Zero-Knowledge In-Browser Document Scanning

Traditional mobile scanner apps often upload personal receipts, tax forms, and driver's licenses to third-party cloud servers, risking sensitive PII data exposure:

  • Local HTML5 Canvas Image Processing: This web scanner processes every pixel entirely on your local graphics processor using HTML5 2D Canvas context. No server connections are initiated during document filtering or PDF rendering.
  • B&W Adaptive Contrast Binarization: When photographing white paper under indoor lighting, uneven shadows degrade readability. Our client-side binarization filter dynamically separates ink characters from ambient page gradients for crisp readability.
  • Embedded Vector PDF Engine: Using the embedded PDF-Lib engine, processed image streams are wrapped into ISO 32000 compliant PDF structures without requiring watermarked freemium subscriptions.

References: W3C HTML Canvas 2D Context Specification; ISO 32000-1 Document Management Portable Document Format.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Does this document scanner upload my files to a server?

No. The scanner operates 100% inside your browser using client-side HTML5 canvas and WebAssembly. Zero bytes of your documents or IDs are ever sent to an external server.

Are there page limits or watermarks on generated PDFs?

No. You can scan and combine unlimited pages into clean, watermark-free, high-resolution PDF documents completely free.

Can I scan multiple receipts or IDs into a single PDF?

Yes. You can upload or capture multiple images, apply document enhancement filters, arrange page order, and export a unified multi-page PDF.

How do document enhancement filters improve readability?

The Black & White and High Contrast filters isolate text characters from shadows and wrinkled paper backgrounds, creating crisp, printer-ready scanned pages.
